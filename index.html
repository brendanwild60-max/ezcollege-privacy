Privacy Policy

EZcollege for iOS · version 1.0 · last updated 24 August 2026

EZcollege does not collect, transmit, or store any of your data. There are no accounts, no servers, and no analytics. This version of the app contains no networking code of any kind.

What stays on your device

Everything. Syllabus files you import, the courses and events extracted from them, your completion checkmarks, reminder settings, and appearance preferences are stored only on your iPhone — inside the app's own storage and a shared container used by the home screen and lock screen widgets.

Syllabus parsing — reading PDFs and Word documents, recognising text in photographs, and detecting dates — runs entirely on the device using Apple's built-in frameworks. No file or extracted text is ever uploaded, because the app makes no network requests.

Camera and photo library

If you photograph a printed syllabus or choose an existing image, that image is read on your device to recognise its text. It is not copied, retained, or sent anywhere. Access is requested only when you use those features, and you can decline and still use the app by importing a file or pasting text.

Notifications

Reminders are scheduled locally by iOS. They are not push notifications, they involve no server, and nothing about them leaves your device.

Bug reports

The “Report a bug” option in Settings opens the Mail app with a message you review and send yourself. Nothing is sent automatically or in the background.

That message includes technical diagnostics:

  • App version, device model, iOS version, and locale
  • How many events exist
  • Whether the shared container resolved, and whether storage fell back

It deliberately does not include course names, assignment titles, dates, or any syllabus text — those are your academic records. If you want to send a syllabus that parsed badly, you attach it yourself.

If no mail account is configured, the report is copied to your clipboard instead, so nothing is ever sent without you.

Data deletion

Deleting the app removes all of its data from your device. There is nothing held elsewhere to request deletion of.

Children

EZcollege is not directed at children under 13, and collects no data from anyone.

Changes to this policy

Any change will be published at this address with an updated date and version. If a future version of the app gains a feature that transmits data — such as optional sync between your phone and a browser — that change will be described here, and will apply only to that version.
